🚀 Get Started: Set Up Your IDS Sheets

What are IDS Sheets?
IDS Sheets are special Google Sheets used to manage your data for "The Tower" to make informed decisions in the game.
  • Effective Paths Sheet: The main sheet that uses your data to calculate your best upgrade path.
  • IDS Master Sheet: The main sheet that links all your data together.
  • Category Sheets: One for each type (Laboratory, Workshop, etc.).
How to set up:
  • Open the IDS Master sheet template and make a copy to your Drive.
  • For each category, open the template and make a copy (All links below):
  • Input your game data for each category
  • Connect your IDS Master sheet in each category sheet.
  • Connect your category sheets in your IDS Master sheet.
  • Open the Effective Paths sheet template and make a copy to your Drive.
  • Connect your IDS Master Sheet in the Effective Paths sheet
  • Now you are ready to use the Effective Paths sheet to optimize your game progress
  • Next time a category sheet gets a new version you can use the import function to transfer your data
